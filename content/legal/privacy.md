## 2. Information We Collect
We collect information that identifies, relates to, or could reasonably be linked directly or indirectly with you or your organization ("Personal Data"). The categories of information we collect include:
- **Direct Inquiry & Contact Details:** Your full name, corporate name, email address, telephone or WhatsApp mobile numbers, business location, and inquiry messages provided when filling out consultation or project booking forms.
- **Project Requirements & Scope:** Details regarding your website requirements, digital marketing objectives, software specifications (ERP, CRM, Billing), existing technology platforms, and ad spend budget ranges.
- **Technical & Usage Information:** Internet Protocol (IP) addresses, browser type, operating system, device identifiers, referring URLs, date/time timestamps, and interaction patterns gathered automatically when browsing our pages.

We use the information we collect for the following legitimate business purposes:
- To evaluate project briefs, formulate scopes of work, and provide accurate proposals or quotations.
- To schedule discovery sessions and strategy phone consultations requested by you.
- To execute digital marketing campaigns (Local SEO, Google Ads, Meta Ads) and custom software development agreements.
- To communicate technical updates, maintenance reports, project milestone deliverables, and account notices.
- To comply with statutory tax (GST), legal, and accounting record-keeping requirements under applicable Indian laws.
- To protect the integrity, security, and performance of our infrastructure against fraudulent activity and unauthorized access.

## 4. Enquiry Forms and Direct Communication
When you submit an inquiry form on `cdmlko.com` or contact us via WhatsApp, your contact details are received directly by our business operations and technical scoping teams. ## 5. Cookies and Automated Tracking Technologies
Our website may employ standard cookies, web beacons, and related tracking scripts to optimize browsing speed, remember interface preferences, and analyze aggregate visitor traffic. Cookies are small text files stored on your local device. You can manage or disable cookie preferences at any time through your browser settings; however, disabling certain functional cookies may impact the proper rendering of specific interactive elements on our website.

---

## 6. Web Analytics and Performance Monitoring
We may utilize privacy-focused web analytics services to measure traffic volume, geographic reach, popular service categories, and navigation funnels across `cdmlko.com`. These analytics tools collect aggregated, anonymized metrics (such as page visit counts and session durations) that do not directly reveal individual customer identities.

## 7. Google & Meta Advertising Integrations
As a digital marketing agency offering Google Ads management and Meta Ads lead generation, we may utilize third-party advertising tracking tags (such as Google Ads Conversion Tracking or Meta Pixel) on select marketing campaign landing pages to monitor conversion performance and evaluate return on ad spend (ROAS). These third-party platforms use their own cookies and identifiers governed by their respective corporate privacy policies.

## 8. Payment Processors and Financial Information
For commercial contracts requiring online milestone billing or retainer invoices, payment transactions may be routed through certified, RBI-compliant third-party payment gateways (such as Razorpay). CODESYNC does not capture, store, or process full credit card numbers, debit card PINs, CVV codes, or net banking credentials on our local servers. All transactional cardholder data is encrypted and handled directly by authorized payment gateways conforming to PCI-DSS standards.

We do not share your personal data with third parties except under the following controlled circumstances:

- **Client Project Tools:** Third-party developer platforms (e.g., domain registrars, cloud servers, SMS/email gateways) configured on your explicit behalf and with your prior authorization.
- **Legal & Regulatory Obligations:** When required by law enforcement authorities, court orders, or statutory regulatory bodies in compliance with the laws of India.
